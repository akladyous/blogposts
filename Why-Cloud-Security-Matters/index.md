### Discover how MDC unifies security and cloud posture to identify vulnerabilities and protect workloads.

#### Here's What Microsoft Defender for Cloud Does About It

If we ever wanted to connect the dots and have a comprehensive, unified platform to secure cloud environments while maintaining a healthy security posture for
an organization, the challenge has always been fragmentation. The big cloud providers each came with their own recipe: their own tools, their own dashboards,
their own definitions of what "secure" even means. Each one competing, each one claiming dominance.

Microsoft did not just want to add another voice to that noise. They wanted to land with impact. So they built something that goes beyond a single cloud. A
comprehensive platform that unifies the major security capabilities across clouds, giving organizations a single place to see, assess, and act.

That platform is **Microsoft Defender for Cloud**. At its core, it operates through three major security pillars, continuously discovering, evaluating, and
prioritizing risk by comparing the actual state of your cloud resources against security best practices, compliance frameworks, and real-world attack logic.

## The three pillars

**Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM)** Think of CSPM as your cloud's health check — running continuously in the background. It scores your environment,
maps out how an attacker could move through your infrastructure, and tells you exactly where you stand against compliance standards like NIST or ISO.

**Development Security Operations (DevSecOps)** Security doesn't start when code hits production — it starts when a developer writes the first line. DevSecOps
brings security into the pipeline early, catching misconfigurations in infrastructure templates, scanning container images for vulnerabilities, and flagging
hardcoded secrets before they become a breach.

**Cloud Workload Protection Platform (CWPP)** Once your workloads are live, CWPP is the watchdog. It monitors your virtual machines, Kubernetes clusters,
databases, and serverless functions in real time — detecting threats, suspicious behavior, and active attacks the moment they happen, not after the damage is
done.

Together, these three pillars are not independent tools bolted together, they are a continuous loop. You assess your posture, secure your pipeline, and protect
your live workloads. Each pillar feeds into the next, and that is what makes Defender for Cloud more than just a product. it is a security strategy.
